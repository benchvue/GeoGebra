Pythagorean Theorem

A visual proof, step by step

Step 1 — The Right Triangle

Right Triangle

A right triangle with a 90° corner at B.


a = 3 base (C→B)
b = 4 height (A→B)
c = 5 hypotenuse (A→C)


The hypotenuse is always opposite the right angle.

Step 2 — Draw Squares on Each Side

Three Squares

A square is drawn on each side of the triangle.


Green on base a (→ down)
Blue on height b (→ right)
Red on hypotenuse c (rotated)


Points D, E, F, G, H, I mark the square corners.

Step 3 — Each Square's Area
Side a
a² = 3²
9
Side b
b² = 4²
16
Hypotenuse c
c² = 5²
25
Step 4 — Label & Add
① a² = 9 ② b² = 16 ③ c² = 25
① 9
+
② 16
=
③ 25

9 + 16 = 25 ✓

Step 5 — The Pythagorean Theorem
a2 + b2 = c2

For any right triangle, the sum of the squares of the two shorter sides always equals the square of the hypotenuse.

Numeric Proof
3² + 4² = 5²
9 + 16 = 25
✓ 25 = 25
